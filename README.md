## 1. VIP Operations — Business Analysis

**Problem:** Outdated VIP statuses can waste resources, misdirect retention efforts and create unnecessary operational costs.

**My solution:** I created a synthetic iGaming dataset, analysed player activity and built a decision-tree classification model to demonstrate automated VIP status filtering.

**Business objective:** Reduce manual work, improve player prioritisation and help departments focus their resources where they matter most.

## 2. WeeklyOps — Data Automation

**Problem:** Manual reporting takes time, creates opportunities for errors and slows down decision-making.

**My solution:** I built an automated iGaming reporting pipeline with data validation, reconciliation and interactive visualisations.

**Business objective:** Replace repetitive reporting tasks with faster, reliable and actionable business information.

## 3. Operator AI Audit — AI Quality Control

**Problem:** AI customer support can produce inaccurate answers, violate company policies and expose operators to unnecessary risks.

**My solution:** I developed an independent AI evaluation framework that tests support responses against defined policies, records findings and generates auditable evidence.

**Business objective:** Help operators identify AI failures before they become customer, compliance or financial problems.
